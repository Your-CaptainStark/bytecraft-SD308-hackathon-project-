#  SkillGap AI

### AI-Powered Skill Gap Analyzer for Career Readiness

SkillGap AI is a web application that helps users understand how prepared they are for a target job role.

Users upload their resume, select a target career role, and receive a skill-gap analysis showing their current skills, required skills, missing skills, and areas that need improvement.

---

##  Problem Statement

Students and job seekers often don't know exactly what skills they need to develop for their desired job role.

They may have a resume containing several technical skills, but they lack a clear comparison between:

- What they currently know
- What their target role requires
- Which skills they are missing
- Which skills they should improve first

This makes career preparation inefficient and confusing.

---

##  Our Solution

SkillGap AI analyzes a user's resume and compares the detected skills against the requirements of a selected target job role.

### The process:

```text
Upload Resume
      ↓
Extract Resume Text
      ↓
Detect Existing Skills
      ↓
Select Target Job Role
      ↓
Compare Current vs Required Skills
      ↓
Calculate Skill Gaps
      ↓
Generate Analysis
